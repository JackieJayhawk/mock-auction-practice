About this simulator
Free bilingual auctioneer training simulator for live bid-calling practice. Practice price increments, bidder switching, auction pacing, and hammer-down sequences in English using CNY or HKD price ladders. Use the built-in prompts as a scaffold, then hide them as your fluency improves.
